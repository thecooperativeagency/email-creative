Brian Harris BMW — Acquire SMS Opt-In

Effective date: July 20, 2026

This page describes how customers provide consent to receive text messages from Brian Harris BMW through the Acquire program (complimentary vehicle appraisal invitations and related service-visit follow-up).

How customers opt in

  1. While visiting Brian Harris BMW for service, the customer sees in-dealership signage with a QR code inviting them to request a complimentary vehicle appraisal.
  2. The customer scans the QR code and opens the public appraisal request form:
    https://brianharrisbmw.com/service-drive-appraisal
  3. The customer voluntarily enters their contact information, including mobile number, checks the required consent box, and submits the form.
  4. By submitting the form, the customer agrees to receive text messages from Brian Harris BMW regarding their appraisal request, service appointment, and related follow-up. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Consent language presented to the customer

By checking the box below, you agree to receive text messages from Brian Harris BMW regarding your complimentary vehicle appraisal request and related follow-up during your service visit. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Terms: https://brianharrisbmw.com/acquire-campaign-terms-and-conditions | Privacy: https://brianharrisbmw.com/acquire-campaign-privacy-policy.

What messages customers receive

  • Complimentary vehicle appraisal invitations related to a service visit
  • Follow-up related to a customer’s appraisal request
  • Help / opt-out confirmation messages when a customer replies HELP or STOP

Message frequency, rates, and help

  • Frequency: Message frequency varies based on the customer’s request and related follow-up needs.
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time.
  • Help: Reply HELP or call Brian Harris BMW at (877) 391-3018.

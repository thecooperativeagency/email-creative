The Ultimate
Sales Process

1

Reception

  • Give a positive first impression!
  • Welcome them.
  • How much time do they have today?
2

Needs Assessment

  • Are you replacing a vehicle?
  • Slow the process down.
  • Who is the car for?
  • Listen to your client.
  • How do you like to purchase?
3

Presentation

  • Customize the presentation.
  • Show features that interest them.
  • What makes this car unique?
4

Demonstration

  • Always drive first!!
  • Show why the car is right for them.
  • Let them enjoy the drive!

Would you like to explore some ownership options?

5

Negotiation

  • Client is getting you and a new car.
  • Keep negotiations open!
  • Hassle-free and upfront.
  • Honesty is paramount.
6

Finance

  • Slow down & be thorough on paperwork.
  • Provide realistic time estimates.
7

Delivery

  • Leave a lasting impression.
  • Prep everything possible beforehand.
8

Follow-Up

  • Keep pipeline full.
  • Stay in front of your client.
  • Be the first call when they shop again.

“Go slow to go fast!”

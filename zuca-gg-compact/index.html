Compatibility quick map

Safe Compact-labeled buys: Compact seat cushion, Compact fenders, Compact rain fly, Compact disc rack, GG Compact insert bag.

Usually cross-compatible (confirm listing): putter pouches / sling, handle grips, phone/cart stud mounts, Looney Bin / bottle holders, foam wheels + covers, utility pouches, umbrella holder, FlighTowel mini, tune-up kit (AT/Compact kit).

Double-check before buying: AT 2" padded seat, AT fenders, AT rain fly, AT disc rack (larger), backpack/trekker-only cushions and fenders, bike attachment (AT-oriented).

Not a substitute for live stock: Prices and backorder status change. Open the linked product page before you commit.
